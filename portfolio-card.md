# Bench: Robotics Simulator

![Bench: building a robot](assets/bench-card.png)

**Build a robot, then find out what you got wrong.**

A robot sandbox that runs in the browser. Pick wheels, gears, sensors, a gripper and a battery, then race it through mazes, hill climbs, drag strips and delivery courses on tile, carpet and wet floors. Weight, grip and gearing all change the result, so every build teaches something.

Built for classrooms: guided lessons, live telemetry, CSV export of every run, and a teacher mode that re-runs student builds to check their claimed times.
